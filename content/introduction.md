# Introduction

Welcome to the Loafer's Guide to the UC Berkeley Campus — here you'll find some architectural history, some explanations of some of the things you might see on campus, and some recommendations about where to go and what to look for.

I started putting this text together while attending graduate school at Berkeley in 1987, and completed it in 1994, at which time I was living in the Midwest and unable to show it to anyone who might have been interested. As I state in the Prolog, this text was dated before it was begun — in the six years between finishing it and revisiting it for the first time several buildings had already been demolished and several more of varying quality and prominence added to the campus. Departments have moved, services have disappeared and others have been created. I'm hoping, though, that the information gathered here will be useful and entertaining to people who either remember what the campus used to be like (or want to know) and people who will find that many of the things I describe can still be discovered and experienced.

Enjoy the Guide, and have fun exploring our beautiful and historic campus.
